# Review Prompts

## Contract review

Verify that exact same-surface control candidates are emitted only when deterministic, that field/control ambiguity remains explicit, and that no raw expression text or private identity is persisted.

## Safety review

Verify that the implementation performs no evaluation, Access execution, COM widening, source-value persistence, or runtime-reachability claim.

## Regression review

Verify deterministic ordering, backward-compatible optional fact properties, rule-backed limitations, and representative before/after evidence.
